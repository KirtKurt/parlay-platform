Require exact game/team identities, a Preview game with the same scheduled start, nine unique original batting slots, and observation before T-45. - Bound all new observations to source timestamps and payload fingerprints in immutable pregame snapshots. Source failures leave observations unavailable. Requests are cached and bounded.
- Added successor experiment `mlb-successor-2026-09-09-team-context-v2`. It adds lineup OPS and three-day bullpen pitch-count gaps, each with a missingness indicator, to market movement and starter rates. Train-only scaling/imputation and chronological whole-slate partitions remain. Team observations are required in train/calibration/selection (100/25/25), as are the existing starter observation minima. Existing development and fresh prospective qualification criteria are unchanged.
- Preserved R8 and the prior successor's records and protocol. The new namespace starts its own development and, only after a qualifying development screen, freezes its own candidate and captures fresh prospective predictions. No old failed test is reopened.
